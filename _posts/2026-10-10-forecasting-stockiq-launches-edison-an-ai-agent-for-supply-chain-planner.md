## Recent publications

#### Seq-Flow: Efficient Probabilistic Forecasting with Self-Rollout Error Control

**Huang, Y.; Govil, S.; Dai, B.; Li, P.** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.10440; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2610.10440`

Introduces a conditional flow approach for updating distributions over future trajectories, aimed at few-step probabilistic forecasting. Practitioner relevance: reports large CRPS gains under tight sampling budgets and robustness over hundreds of sequential updates, making it relevant for operational probabilistic forecasting systems.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10440)

#### Just Weather Scoring: Efficient End-to-end Nowcasting with Distributional Diffusion

**Wiese, J.; Schusterbauer, J.; Martorella, T.; Ommer, B.** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.12189; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2610.12189`

Presents a single-stage diffusion model for probabilistic precipitation nowcasting, trained directly in radar space with a scoring-rule objective. Practitioner relevance: claims state-of-the-art probabilistic performance on SEVIR and MeteoNet with reduced inference cost, including a compact model reported as more than 17 times faster.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12189)

#### AdaCast: Conditional Parameter Generation for Adaptive Time Series Forecasting

**Nallagatla, D.; Jacob, D. C.; He, P.** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.12240; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2610.12240`

Proposes input-specific low-rank parameter updates for frozen pretrained time-series foundation models instead of relying on a single static adaptation. Practitioner relevance: targets heterogeneous time series and reports improved in-domain and zero-shot performance across six public benchmarks, which is useful for adapting forecasting models across varied business or operational datasets.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12240)

#### EvoCast: Reliable Autonomous Research Agents for Iterative Forecasting Architecture Evolution

**Xu, K.; Yan, X.; Wang, Y.; Liu, X.; Liu, S.** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.04517; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2610.04517`

Practitioner relevance: focuses on reliable experimental protocol and automated model evolution, reporting task-specific architectures that outperform selected baselines in three real-world forecasting cases.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04517)

#### The effect of local forecasting improvements on supply chain performance

**Cui, G.; Imura, N.; Nishinari, K.; Ezaki, T.** · *European Journal of Industrial Engineering, 22(3), 361–388, 2026* · `DOI: 10.1504/EJIE.2026.156565`

Studies demand forecasting improvements in a serial five-echelon supply chain and shows that better local forecast accuracy can worsen upstream performance if it increases order fluctuations or bullwhip effects. Practitioner relevance: useful for demand-planning teams because it argues forecast evaluation should include supply-chain stability metrics, not just local error reduction.
