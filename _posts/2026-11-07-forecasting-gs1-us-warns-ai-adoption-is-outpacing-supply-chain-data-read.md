## Industry news

### GS1 US warns AI adoption is outpacing supply chain data readiness *(PRNewswire / GS1 US)*

GS1 US released “The Supply Chain Readiness Gap,” finding that many companies are investing in AI, machine learning, and automation before fixing core data integration problems. Nearly 60% of surveyed organizations are using advanced automation or AI/ML in supply chain processes, but only 38% report centralized, real-time data integration, highlighting a major constraint on scalable forecasting and planning improvements.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/gs1-us-study-finds-ai-and-automation-investments-are-outpacing-supply-chain-data-readiness-302927317.html)

### Transportation leaders cite demand forecasting as a top peak-season readiness gap *(Supply & Demand Chain Executive)*

The notable finding is that many organizations already have forecasting tools deployed, suggesting the problem is less about software adoption and more about data quality, integration, process ownership, and execution discipline.
