[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40265)

#### XMatch: Enhancing Covariate-Aware Time Series Forecasting through Tree-Structured Exogenous Matching

**Ziyang Zhang, Hanyin Cheng, Xiangfei Qiu, Yang Shu, Bin Yang, Chenjuan Guo** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2609.34939; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2609.34939`

XMatch improves covariate-aware time-series forecasting by matching future exogenous-variable patterns to historical analogs using a tree-structured mechanism. This is especially relevant for applied forecasting settings involving promotions, weather, calendars, prices, or macroeconomic covariates.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34939)

This paper proposes an inference-time latent guidance method for adapting and combining outputs from time-series foundation models. It is useful for practitioners who want ensemble-like behavior or improved robustness without fully retraining large forecasting models.
