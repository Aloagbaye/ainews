transcosmos expanded trans-scManager beyond inventory visualization into demand forecasting, replenishment planning, and inventory allocation optimization. ## Recent publications

#### A Deep Learning Framework for Quantile Demand Forecasting in E-commerce with Doubly Censored Data

**Ziwen Ye, Jiawen Wei, Teng Song, Chen Chen, Guangrui Ma** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.40138`

Introduces a deep learning framework for quantile demand forecasting in e-commerce settings where observed sales are doubly censored by stockouts and promotions. Practitioner relevance is high for retail inventory planning because the method targets probabilistic demand estimates under common real-world data limitations.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40138)

#### FARE: Factor-Aware Retrieval with Error-Guided Fusion for Retail Forecasting

**Chenghao Gu, Zhuokang Shen, Shenghao Wu, Tingxuan Zhu, Weiyi Luo, Haifeng Tang** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.40160`

Proposes a factor-aware retrieval and error-guided fusion approach for retail forecasting, combining backbone forecasts with historical references selected by shape and business factors. The work is directly relevant to demand planning and replenishment, with reported improvements on retail benchmarks such as Favorita, M5, Rossmann, and Rohlik.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40160)

#### IMRouter: Imbalance-aware Temporal Pattern Routing for LLM-based Time Series Forecasting

**Xin Yang, Cai Xu, Haishun Chen, Ying Liu, Jie Zhao, Wei Zhao, Ziyu Guan** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.39246`

Addresses temporal pattern imbalance in LLM-based time-series forecasting using an imbalance-aware routing mechanism and semantic alignment. This is relevant for practitioners deploying foundation-model-style forecasters in operational domains where rare temporal regimes can drive failures or high costs.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39246)

#### FACTS-MAS: A Multi-Agent Architecture for Adaptive Forecasting from Heterogeneous Evidence

**Madumita Karthikeyan, Umang Rajnikant Bid, Rong Pan** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.38225`

Presents a multi-agent forecasting architecture that combines heterogeneous evidence sources such as temporal dynamics, seasonality, events, exogenous context, and structural signals. It is useful for practitioners interested in adaptive ensemble forecasting, since agents are weighted based on historical validation skill and evaluated across real economic and housing time series.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38225)

#### TimeWorld: Towards Action-Conditioned Multimodal World Models for Time Series Forecasting

**Hang Ni, Weijia Zhang, Hao Liu** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.34797`

Introduces action-conditioned multimodal world models for time-series forecasting, focusing on settings where planned interventions or actions influence future trajectories. This is practically relevant for forecasting under promotions, operations changes, or control policies where standard passive forecasting can miss action-outcome effects.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34797)
