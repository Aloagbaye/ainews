### Nvidia CEO transition rattles the AI hardware stack `GPU & Hardware`

Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO, and board member, with CFO Colette Kress appointed interim CEO while remaining CFO. For AI builders, the significance is less the executive drama than the uncertainty around the company that anchors most frontier-model training and inference capacity.

[Source](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)

### OpenAI turns ChatGPT into a commerce surface `Systems Design`

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT on November 10, letting companies claim profiles, manage information, view engagement insights, and—for eligible U.S. ecommerce and online-service businesses—publish labeled Business Agents. It followed with ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, signaling a move toward user-approved agentic checkout inside ChatGPT rather than simple outbound referrals.

[Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)

### Google widens Gemini 4: Carbon for frontier work, Hydrogen for fast agent loops `Cost & Efficiency`

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon broadly available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers, positioning it for long-horizon coding, cybersecurity, and scientific reasoning. Google also introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, aimed at faster everyday coding and agentic workflows across Gemini API, AI Studio, Search, and other products.

[Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)

### AI-assisted formal verification gets real with Signal `Research & Methodology`

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, a multi-agent human–AI framework for formal software specification and verification, and used it to prove key security properties of Signal’s libsignal implementation at the machine-code level. The important technical signal is that frontier models are being applied not just to write code, but to produce machine-checkable proofs about deployed security-critical systems.

[Source](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/)

### Inference engineering shifts toward KV-cache movement and cheaper fleets `Systems Design`

Google Cloud introduced TPU Sync, an open-source C++ library for fast KV-cache and weight transfer on TPUs, targeting multi-tier cache movement across HBM, host DRAM, SSD, storage, and network paths. AWS also published an AssemblyAI case study showing 70% inference-compute savings versus On-Demand pricing by running production speech-to-text workloads on EC2 Spot GPU instances with retry architecture.

[Source](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/ai-infrastructure/introducing-tpu-sync-fast-kv-cache-and-weight-transfer-on-tpu) · [Source](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-assemblyai-reduced-inference-costs-by-70-with-amazon-ec2-spot-instances/)
