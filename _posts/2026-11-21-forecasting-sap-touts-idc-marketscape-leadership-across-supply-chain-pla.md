## Industry news

### SAP touts IDC MarketScape leadership across supply chain planning *(SAP News Center)*

SAP said it was named a Leader in IDC’s 2026 Worldwide Supply Chain Planning Overall Vendor Assessment, along with industry-specific planning assessments for process industries, life sciences, discrete industries and CPG. The announcement positions supply chain planning as a connected decision layer spanning demand, supply, inventory and execution rather than a standalone forecasting tool.

[Source](https://news.sap.com/2026/11/sap-named-leader-idc-marketscape-supply-chain-assessments/)

### ketteQ launches Ask Quintus to let planners test an AI planning agent *(ketteQ)*

ketteQ launched Ask Quintus, an online experience that lets supply chain and planning professionals ask planning questions and see how the Quintus AI agent reasons through scenarios using representative industry data. The launch reflects growing buyer demand to distinguish genuine planning-agent capabilities from scripted demos or conventional workflow automation.

[Source](https://www.ketteq.com/blog/ketteq-launches-ask-quintus-giving-supply-chain-teams-a-hands-on-way-to-try-an-ai-planning-agent)

### LEAFIO AI releases Forecast Assistant for explainable retail demand forecasting *(LEAFIO AI)*

LEAFIO AI announced Forecast Assistant, a conversational layer in LEAFIO Inventory Optimization that explains base demand forecasts. The tool helps retailers identify forecast drivers such as trend, seasonality, lifecycle stage, historical demand and stockout effects before forecasts feed replenishment, safety stock, allocation and purchasing decisions.

[Source](https://www.leafio.ai/news/leafio-ai-forecast-assistant-the-next-step-in-applying-ai-agents-to-retail-operations/)

### Körber study says AI is spreading, but decision-making is getting harder *(PR Newswire / Körber AG)*

Körber released its AI and Decision Complexity Report, based on 2,000 senior decision-makers in the U.S. and Germany. The report found that 77% of U.S. respondents said AI is increasingly involved in decision-making, while 78% said decision-making has become more complex, with supply chain leaders showing especially deep AI involvement.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ai-investment-is-widespread-but-3-in-4-leaders-say-decision-making-is-now-more-complex-302942075.html)

### Models at Work finds supply chain AI results are still hard to price *(Models at Work)*

Models at Work analyzed ten recent supply chain and procurement AI deployments across companies including C.H. Robinson, Genpact, GXO, XPO, Sysco, Costco, Bristol Myers Squibb, Asics America, Fura and Montgomery County Food Bank. The analysis found that while companies reported operational gains in metrics such as miles, days, inventory counts and overtime, none disclosed the AI tool’s license, usage or build cost.

[Source](https://modelsatwork.news/article/supply-chain-ai-tally-november-2026-ten-deployments-no-ai-price-two-filings)

## Recent publications

#### Conformal Expert Pools: Training-Free Probabilistic Time-Series Forecasting Beyond Seasonality

**V. Manokhin** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2611.21267; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2611.21267`

Proposes a training-free probabilistic time-series forecasting approach that pools simple experts and constructs predictive distributions from past forecast errors. Practitioner relevance is high as a lightweight, calibrated baseline for demand and operational forecasting, with reported improvements on CRPS and interval scores over several probabilistic baselines.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21267)

#### Forecast Value Add: Reward-Based Action Selection over Probabilistic Demand Forecasts

**A. Verma** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2611.12116; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2611.12116`

Introduces a reward-based action-selection layer over probabilistic demand forecast candidates such as base forecasts, bounds, quantiles, and bias-corrected estimates. The method is directly relevant to retail and consumer-goods forecasting, with deployment results reporting WMAPE reductions across multiple commercial settings.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12116)

#### GLIDE: Scalable and Event-Aware Inductive Forecasting over Evolving Logistics Graphs

**Z. Sheng, C. Wang, J. Ji, Y. Liang, X. Ding, J. Hao, J. Feng, and Y. Li** · *KDD 2027 / arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2611.23942; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2611.23942`

Presents an event-aware inductive forecasting model for evolving logistics graphs, targeting order-volume prediction in dynamic food-delivery and logistics networks. It is relevant for supply-chain and dispatch operations because it addresses changing graph topology, long-tailed demand, and disruptions from events such as holidays and weather.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23942)

#### SLiDE: Structured Linear Dynamics for Forecasting with Exogenous Inputs

**S. Pütz, T. Glavas, B. Schäfer, B. N. Oreshkin, and M. Coates** · *NeurIPS 2026* · `arXiv:2611.19586; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2611.19586`

Develops a Koopman-inspired neural forecasting architecture with structured latent linear dynamics driven by future exogenous inputs. The approach is useful for operational settings such as energy, prices, weather-driven forecasts, and retail demand where future covariates are available and distribution shifts in exogenous inputs matter.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19586)

#### EPI-SST: Renewal-Structured State-Space Transformers for Epidemic Forecasting

**Y. Chen, Z. Liu, S. Tang, M. S. Y. Lau, and W. Jin** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2611.20352; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2611.20352`

Combines epidemic renewal structure with a state-space Transformer and probabilistic uncertainty head for calibrated multi-horizon epidemic forecasts. The paper is relevant to public-health forecasting workflows because it emphasizes both accuracy and uncertainty metrics, including RMSE and CRPS, on epidemic surveillance benchmarks.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20352)
