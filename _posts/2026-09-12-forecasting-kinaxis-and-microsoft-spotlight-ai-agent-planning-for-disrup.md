### Descartes says August U.S. container imports hit third-highest monthly level on record *(Descartes)*

The report also flags rising transit delays at major U.S. gateways, trade-policy risk, Panama Canal constraints, and Red Sea/Middle East disruption risk.

[Source](https://www.descartes.com/resources/knowledge-center/descartes-releases-september-global-shipping-report-august-us)

### Deloitte forecasts 2026 holiday retail sales at $1.70T–$1.71T *(Deloitte via PRNewswire)*

Deloitte projects U.S. holiday retail sales of $1.70 trillion to $1.71 trillion for November 2026 through January 2027, up 4.0% to 4.8% year over year. E-commerce is forecast to grow faster, rising 7.5% to 8.4% and reaching $316.1 billion to $318.9 billion.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/deloitte-forecasts-holiday-retail-sales-to-reach-1-70-trillion-to-1-71-trillion-302874356.html)

### Maersk urges earlier forecasts and scenario planning for North America peak exposure *(Maersk)*

Maersk’s September North America market update says peak season is unfolding amid tight capacity, trade-policy changes, Asian weather delays, low-water issues, and technology-driven demand across ocean and air freight. The company advises shippers to share forecasts early, book ahead for Asian origins, model peak exposure against surcharge schedules, and keep routing and gateway options flexible.

[Source](https://www.maersk.com/news/articles/2026/09/09/north-america-market-update-september)
