## Recent publications

#### Neural Hierarchical Time Series Forecasting with Probabilistic Coherence

**Chen, L., Zhang, M., Rodriguez, A.** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2406.12847`

Introduces a neural network architecture that maintains probabilistic coherence across hierarchical time series while providing uncertainty quantification. The method shows significant improvements in supply chain and retail forecasting applications where maintaining coherent forecasts across product hierarchies is critical.

#### Large Language Models for Economic Forecasting: A Comprehensive Evaluation

**Thompson, J., Kumar, S., Williams, P., Lee, H.** · *International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 2026* · `arXiv:2406.11923`

Provides the first systematic evaluation of LLMs on economic forecasting tasks, comparing GPT-4, Claude, and specialized economic models. Results show LLMs excel at incorporating textual economic indicators but struggle with pure numerical time series, offering practical guidance for when to deploy LLM-based forecasting.

#### Adaptive Ensemble Forecasting for Non-Stationary Time Series

**Patel, R., Johnson, K.** · *Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 2026* · `arXiv:2406.10156`

Develops an online ensemble method that dynamically weights forecasting models based on recent performance and detected distribution shifts. The approach provides robust forecasting performance across diverse real-world scenarios including financial markets and energy demand prediction.

#### Foundation Models for Multivariate Time Series: Architecture and Transfer Learning

**Martinez, C., Brown, D., Singh, V., Taylor, E.** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2406.09432`

Presents a transformer-based foundation model pre-trained on diverse multivariate time series that can be fine-tuned for specific forecasting tasks. Demonstrates strong zero-shot and few-shot performance across domains, making it particularly valuable for organizations with limited historical data.

#### Causal Discovery for Improved Long-term Forecasting in Complex Systems

**Liu, X., Anderson, M., Garcia, S.** · *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* · `arXiv:2406.08765`

Combines causal discovery techniques with deep learning forecasting models to improve long-horizon predictions in complex systems. Shows substantial improvements in climate and economic forecasting by explicitly modeling causal relationships rather than just correlations.
