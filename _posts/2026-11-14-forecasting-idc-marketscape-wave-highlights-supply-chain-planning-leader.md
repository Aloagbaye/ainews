## Recent publications

#### Koopman Generative Operators for Efficient Probabilistic Time-Series Forecasting

**Raz Marshanski, Liran Nochumsohn, Mayank Jauhari, Boris N. Oreshkin, Omri Azencot** · *NeurIPS 2026 / arXiv preprint, submitted Nov. 3, 2026* · `arXiv:2611.04760; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2611.04760`

Proposes Koopman Generative Operators, combining Koopman latent dynamics, flow matching, and adaptive uncertainty gating for efficient probabilistic time-series forecasting. Practitioner relevance is high for teams needing calibrated probabilistic forecasts with improved CRPS/NMAE and faster inference than iterative generative approaches.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04760)

#### TimeHF 2.0: Multimodal, Multivariate, and Multi-Region Collaborative Forecasting for Large-Scale Retail Supply Chains

**Zhongxuan Han, Zhengxin Shi, Kunxiu Chen, Xiaolin Huang, Yulin Huang, Anni Zhang, Xiaoming Lin, Zhengyu Chen, Xue Yu, Yang Liu, Yuanyuan Yang, Yangyang Zhang, Junheng Huang, Dongyang Geng, Ben Zhang, Xin Wang, Jiaxin Wang, Haowei Zhou, Qiqi Yan, Jiaheng Yin, Jintao Xu, Zijiang Liu, Changfei Zhan, Zhe Chen, Yutong Pan, Jinqiu Deng, Shaomin Li, Ningxuan Kang, Jianshen Zhang, Yongzhi Qi, Wei Qi, Dazhou Lei, Zuo-Jun Max Shen** · *Technical report / arXiv preprint, submitted Nov. 7, 2026* · `arXiv:2611.10521; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2611.10521`

Introduces TimeHF 2.0 for large-scale retail supply-chain forecasting using multimodal, multivariate, and multi-region signals, including sales, covariates, business knowledge, language-model hidden states, routed/shared experts, and an ordered-distribution head. It is especially relevant for retail and supply-chain practitioners because it reports online deployment improvements at JD.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10521)

#### Forecast Value Add: Reward-Based Action Selection over Probabilistic Demand Forecasts

**Ankur Verma** · *arXiv preprint, submitted Nov. 9, 2026* · `arXiv:2611.12116; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2611.12116`

Frames forecast adjustment as reward-based action selection over probabilistic demand-forecast ensembles, using distribution-free bounds, quantiles, and bias-corrected estimates. This is useful for practitioners in retail, consumer goods, and DTC settings who need systematic ways to decide when forecast overrides or ensemble actions add value.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12116)

#### Learning Evidence-Grounded Reasoning for Context-Aware Time Series Forecasting

**Xingpeng Gao, Mingyue Cheng, Daoyu Wang, Huajian Zhang, Xiaoyu Tao** · *arXiv preprint, submitted Nov. 9, 2026* · `arXiv:2611.12285; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2611.12285`

It is relevant to practitioners exploring LLM-assisted forecasting because it combines supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning post-training, and multi-objective rewards.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12285)

#### CATF: A Manager-Worker Framework for Context-Aware Time-Series Forecasting

**Yueqiao Chen, Dongyu Liu** · *arXiv preprint, submitted Nov. 3, 2026* · `arXiv:2611.05325; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2611.05325`

Proposes CATF, a manager-worker framework where a Transformer-based manager routes context-rich inputs to specialized worker forecasters. The method is practically relevant as an interpretable mixture-of-experts-style ensemble for context-aware forecasting, reporting improvements over standalone baselines across many settings.
