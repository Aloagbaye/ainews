### Anthropic files for IPO under ticker ANT `News`

Anthropic filed its Form S-1 with the SEC on October 26, 2026, aiming to list Class A common stock on Nasdaq under “ANT.” The filing marks a major shift for frontier AI labs, moving Anthropic from private mega-financing into public-market scrutiny around compute spend, enterprise revenue, safety risk, and model economics.

[Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec)

### OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra as a cheaper high-volume agent model `Cost & Efficiency`

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Terra as a fast, efficient model for visual reasoning, computer use, writing, and work automation. The key cost signal is pricing: $1 per million input tokens, $0.05 per million cached input tokens, and $5 per million output tokens, which OpenAI says is 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol.

[Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra/)

### Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon for cyber and long-horizon agent work `Research & Methodology`

Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28, describing it as its strongest model with emphasis on cybersecurity, coding, and long-horizon agentic workflows. Rollout is initially limited to cyber defenders and trusted partners while the model participates in U.S. pre-release testing; introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached inputs discounted 95%.

[Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)

### Google Research proposes a “sleep” phase for continual LLM learning `Research & Methodology`

Google Research published a continual-learning framework that alternates LLMs between wake phases for interaction and sleep phases for offline consolidation into model weights. The approach combines ideas such as MoE memory blocks, parameter expansion, generalized knowledge distillation, RL-based imitation, synthetic dream data, and pruning to reduce catastrophic forgetting.

[Source](https://research.google/blog/the-sleep-framework-a-new-way-for-llms-to-retain-knowledge-in-continual-learning/?trk=article-ssr-frontend-pulse_little-text-block)

### Meta and AMD deepen rack-scale AI infrastructure partnership `GPU & Hardware`

AMD said its Helios rackscale solutions are running at Meta, with collaboration across CPUs, GPUs, networking, systems, ROCm, PyTorch, and workload-specific silicon. The systems-design signal is Meta’s move toward rack-scale and gigawatt-scale AI infrastructure, including custom AMD Instinct GPUs optimized for Meta workloads and day-0 support for Meta models.

[Source](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1302/amd-and-meta-advance-strategic-partnership-to-power-metas-superintelligence-infrastructure)
