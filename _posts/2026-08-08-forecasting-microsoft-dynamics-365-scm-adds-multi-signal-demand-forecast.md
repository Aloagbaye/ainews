## Recent publications

#### TS-RAG: Retrieval Augmented Generation for Time Series Forecasting

**Yixiong Xiao, Congxi Xiao, Jingbo Zhou** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2608.06223; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2608.06223`

Introduces a retrieval-augmented generation approach for time series forecasting, retrieving similar historical sequences and integrating them as reference tokens. This is relevant for practitioners exploring RAG-style forecasting systems, especially where similar-pattern retrieval can improve accuracy and interpretability.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06223)

#### FedChronos: Federated Fine-Tuning of Time-Series Foundation Models for Privacy-Preserving Commodity Price Forecasting

**Amit Sharma, Nitin Auluck, Akramul Azim** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2608.01290; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2608.01290`

Presents a federated fine-tuning framework for adapting Chronos-T5 to commodity price forecasting while preserving data privacy. The work is practically relevant for organizations that need collaborative forecasting across distributed or sensitive datasets, using LoRA, FedAvg/FedProx, and differential privacy.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01290)

**Thomas Bartz-Beielstein** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2608.05018; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2608.05018`

Studies short-term load forecasting in a compliance-oriented, safety-critical setting using a live German transmission-grid forecasting challenge. #### Schedule-Informed Temporal Fusion Forecasting of Hourly Airport Security-Checkpoint Throughput

**Yinxiao Zhang, Sen Wang, Yi Gao** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2608.02950; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2608.02950`
