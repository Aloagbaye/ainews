#### FlowTSFM: Turning Encoder Depth into Quantile Transport

**Bahaeddine Abdessalem, Shifeng Xie, Zehao Xiao, Youssef Attia El Hili, Ambroise Odonnat, Jianfeng Zhang, Lujia Pan, Keli Zhang, Malik Tiomoko** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.13640; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2609.13640`

This paper proposes a time-series foundation-model architecture that treats encoder depth as a quantile-transport process, using a quantile-flow objective with pinball loss and intermediate path supervision. The approach targets parameter-efficient probabilistic forecasting, which is relevant for practitioners deploying uncertainty-aware forecasting models at scale.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13640)

#### Coherence is not enough: Aggregation constraints across predictive distributions, forecasts and decisions

**Shun Hu, Yanfei Kang** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.15167; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2609.15167`

It is important for practitioners because it shows that distributional coherence alone may not ensure coherent operational decisions, such as inventory decisions under loss functions.
