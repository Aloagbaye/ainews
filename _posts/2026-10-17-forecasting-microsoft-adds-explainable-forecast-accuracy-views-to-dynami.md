## Recent publications

#### PathFM: Fast Generative Forecasting of Multivariate Trajectories

**Jiachen Yao, Xiyuan Zhang, Annan Yu, Boran Han, Abdul Fatir Ansari, Shuai Zhang, Michael Bohlke-Schneider, Bernie Wang** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.22358; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2610.22358`

PathFM uses flow matching to generate complete future multivariate trajectories, focusing on dependence across forecast horizons rather than only marginal predictive distributions. This is relevant for practitioners who need coherent scenarios for cumulative demand, peak-load planning, and event-timing decisions.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22358)

#### Quantile-Guided Diffusion for Probabilistic Time-Series Forecasting

**Shadi Omari, Bashar Khoury, Assaf Schuster** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.20739`

The paper proposes Control-TS, a diffusion-based probabilistic time-series forecasting approach guided by predicted quantiles while keeping the pretrained backbone frozen. It is useful for practitioners interested in calibrated scenario generation and probabilistic forecast quality, with reported CRPS improvements across multiple benchmarks.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20739)

#### Deterministic Prediction as Global Velocity: Flow Matching for Probabilistic Time-Series Forecast

**Jiajing Hu, Xu Han, Li-Ping Liu** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2610.18621; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2610.18621`

This work introduces DV-Flow, a flow-matching method that incorporates a deterministic predictor as a parallel velocity component for probabilistic time-series forecasting. The approach is practitioner-relevant because it aims to combine stable point-forecast structure with generative uncertainty modeling across real-world forecasting datasets.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18621)
