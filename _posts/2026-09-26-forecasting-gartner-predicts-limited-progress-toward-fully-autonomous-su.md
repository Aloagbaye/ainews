## Industry news

### Gartner predicts limited progress toward fully autonomous supply-chain planning *(Gartner)*

Gartner predicted that by 2030, only 5% of organizations implementing supply-chain planning automation will make at least 10% of planning decisions autonomously. The firm says many companies are already investing heavily in AI and planning automation, but data quality, governance, technology maturity, workforce readiness and decision ownership remain major barriers.

[Source](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-09-24-gartner-predicts-only-5-percent-of-organizations-will-make-at-least-10-percent-of-supply-chain-planning-decisions-autonomously-by-2030)

### Amazon adds AI supply-chain agents for seller planning *(Supply Chain Dive)*

Amazon is adding agentic supply-chain capabilities to Seller Assistant, including tools for inbound planning and aged inventory. The move pushes AI-enabled planning workflows deeper into marketplace operations, giving sellers proactive recommendations around inventory timing, stock health and global selling decisions.

[Source](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/amazon-ai-supply-chain-agents-among-seller-upgrades/831164/)

### Netstock benchmark says SMBs face broader ‘supply chain chaos’ *(Netstock)*

Netstock’s 2026 Benchmark Report says SMBs are no longer planning around a single dominant disruption, but around overlapping pressures including supplier volatility, demand shifts, freight costs, raw-material prices and inventory performance. The report also finds growing interest in AI investment, underscoring the need to model supply variability alongside demand signals.

[Source](https://www.netstock.com/blog/netstock-benchmark-report-2026/) · [Source](https://www.netstock.com/research/supply-chain-planning-report/)

### Kroger outlines AI strategy across forecasting, operations and agentic workflows *(The Kroger Co.)*

Kroger detailed an enterprise AI strategy at GroceryShop 2026 built on customer insights, operational data, proprietary models and governance guardrails. The company says its AI portfolio includes demand forecasting, operational optimization, personalization and generative AI, with a staged path from assisted to augmented and eventually autonomous workflows.

[Source](https://www.thekrogerco.com/ir-feed-item/kroger-details-ai-strategy-built-on-decades-retail-expertise-at-groceryshop-2026/)

### ISG says multi-agent orchestration is emerging in AI supply-chain planning *(ISG Research)*

ISG Research argued that AI-enabled supply-chain planning is moving beyond copilots toward multi-agent orchestration across demand, supply, inventory and finance workflows. The analysis emphasizes observability, traceability, human-in-the-loop controls and measurable outcomes such as forecast improvement, lower transport costs and better service levels.

[Source](https://research.isg-one.com/analyst-perspectives/the-state-of-ai-and-agents-in-supply-chain-planning)

## Recent publications

#### TimeBraid: Unifying Time Series and Language for Understanding and Forecasting

**Xinyue Wang, Jiacheng Pang, Kun Zhou, Kexin Zhang, Defu Cao, Fan Feng, Faisal, Songyao Jin, Yan Liu, Biwei Huang** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.29792; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2609.29792`

Introduces TimeBraid, a framework that aligns pretrained language models with pretrained time-series foundation models using interleaved residual attention. It targets both time-series understanding/reasoning and forecasting, including context-aided forecasting, making it relevant for practitioners exploring LLM-assisted forecasting workflows.

#### t₀: A Time-Series Foundation Model for Forecasting with Context

**Lucas Meyer, Claudio Sole, Huikan Xiang, Nicolas Li, Lucas Franceschino, Arnau Quera-Bofarull, Maarten P. Scholl, Joachim Fainberg, Geoffrey Négiar** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.24559; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2609.24559`

Presents open-weights time-series foundation models that condition on target history, past covariates, and known-future covariates. The models generate probabilistic forecasts via quantile predictions and report strong benchmark results, including practical relevance for electricity-demand forecasting.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24559) · [Link 2](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.24559)

#### fable.intermittent: benchmarking probabilistic forecasting methods for intermittent time series

**Stefano Damato, Lorenzo Zambon, Giorgio Corani, Dario Azzimonti** · *arXiv preprint; submitted to the International Journal of Forecasting, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.28607; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2609.28607`

Introduces fable.intermittent, an R package for benchmarking probabilistic forecasting methods on intermittent time series. The paper also proposes a Tweedie exponential-smoothing model, TWEES, with direct practitioner relevance for spare-parts demand, retail sales, and other intermittent-demand settings.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28607) · [Link 2](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.28607)

#### Downside-Controlled Online Forecast Combination under Delayed and Revised Outcomes

**Minkyoung Kim, Hyunjung Byun, Yohan Lee, Beakcheol Jang** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.29096; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2609.29096`

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29096) · [Link 2](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.29096)

#### SGA: Uncertainty Quantification for Multi-Step Forecasting in Time Series Foundation Models

**Xin-Yu Hu, Shuang Liang, Cheng Feng, Shao-Qun Zhang** · *arXiv preprint, 2026* · `arXiv:2609.28582; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2609.28582`

Introduces Slicing–Graphing–Alignment, an uncertainty-quantification method for multi-step forecasts from time-series foundation models. It models forecast-branch topology with a directed acyclic graph and evaluates across many foundation models and datasets, making it useful for practitioners needing calibrated multi-step forecast uncertainty.
