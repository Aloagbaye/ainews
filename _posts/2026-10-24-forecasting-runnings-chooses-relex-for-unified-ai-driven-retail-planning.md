## Industry news

### Runnings chooses RELEX for unified AI-driven retail planning *(PRNewswire / RELEX Solutions)*

Runnings, a U.S. farm, home and outdoor retailer with 90 stores across 12 states, selected RELEX to strengthen store and distribution-center planning. The rollout starts with forecasting and replenishment, then expands into pricing, promotion, assortment, space and floor planning, underscoring the shift toward a single shared demand forecast across retail planning functions.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/runnings-enhances-supply-chain-and-retail-planning-with-relex-302917536.html)

### Blue Ridge launches AI-native supply chain planning platform *(Blue Ridge Global)*

Blue Ridge launched a next-generation AI-native planning platform for distributors, manufacturers and retailers, built on Databricks and positioned around “Supply Chain Intelligence.” The announcement emphasizes explainable planning decisions, an Adaptive ML Forecast Engine, and connected workflows for demand forecasting, replenishment and inventory optimization.

[Source](https://www.blueridgeglobal.com/news/blue-ridge-launches-next-generation-platform)

### Wiegand-Glas selects Elisa Industriq/sedApta to modernize production planning across four plants *(PRNewswire / Elisa Oyj)*

German glass manufacturer Wiegand-Glas selected Elisa Industriq’s sedApta supply chain planning solutions for production planning and scheduling across four manufacturing sites. The deployment is intended to improve demand planning, inventory management, production planning and scheduling, with scenario simulation to balance customer demand, inventory and capacity.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wiegand-glas-selects-elisa-industriq-to-modernize-production-planning-across-four-manufacturing-sites-302913559.html)

### AGR analysis says manufacturers and retailers lag in AI adoption as supply chain cost pressure rises *(PRNewswire / AGR Dynamics)*

AGR’s analysis of UK Office for National Statistics data says AI adoption among UK businesses reached 34.7% in June 2026, while manufacturing stood at 26.9% and wholesale/retail at 28.4%. AGR argues many firms are still using generic AI for content tasks rather than operational forecasting and inventory planning, despite rising concern about sourcing, transport and supply chain costs.

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-and-retail-are-falling-behind-on-ai-as-supply-chain-costs-are-expected-to-rise-according-to-analysis-by-agr-302913129.html)

### Snowflake finance team reports cutting monthly forecasting from over a week to hours with AI *(Snowflake)*

Snowflake published an internal business forecasting case study describing how its sales finance team reduced monthly forecast and variance-analysis work from more than a week to a few hours using AI. Although focused on finance rather than supply chain, the case highlights a broader forecasting trend: AI value depends on governed business context, reusable workflows and trusted data models, not just ad hoc prompting.

[Source](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/sales-finance-ai-forecast/)

#### Dynamic Dual-Feedback Conformal Inference for Time Series Forecasting

**Songlin Du, Ling Luo, Uwe Aickelin** · *NeurIPS 2026; arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2610.26987; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2610.26987`

Proposes Dynamic Dual-Feedback Conformal Inference (DDCI), an online conformal inference method for calibrated time-series prediction intervals. It is practically relevant for production forecasting systems that need stable uncertainty estimates, near-nominal coverage, and narrower intervals under distributional change.

#### WhatIf-Bench: Benchmarking Intervention-Aware Time Series Forecasting

**Arjun Ashok, Mihir Parmar, Palash Goyal, Bhavana Dalvi Mishra, Chun-Liang Li, Irina Rish, Alexandre Drouin, Jinsung Yoon, Tomas Pfister** · *arXiv preprint* · `arXiv:2610.26582; DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2610.26582`

Introduces WhatIf-Bench, a benchmark for forecasting target time series under future covariate interventions, with tasks from domains such as data centers, power grids, road traffic, and water networks. The benchmark is useful for practitioners evaluating whether LLMs, time-series foundation models, or classical baselines can support scenario planning and intervention-aware forecasting.

[Read paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26582) · [Link 2](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2610.26582)

Studies agentic model orchestration for time-series forecasting, focusing on when selecting or combining foundation models truly improves performance.
