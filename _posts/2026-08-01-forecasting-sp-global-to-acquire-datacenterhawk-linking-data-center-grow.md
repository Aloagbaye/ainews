#### Forecasting the Number of Harvest-ready Fruits of Sweet Peppers Using Multimodal Time-Series Data

**Enrico Pallotta, Mohamed Farag, Esra Guclu, Chris McCool, Ribana Roscher, Juergen Gall** · *arXiv preprint, cs.CV, submitted July 22, 2026* · `arXiv:2607.19975; DOI:10.48550/arXiv.2607.19975`

Presents a multimodal time-series forecasting method for predicting the number of harvest-ready sweet peppers by combining image time series and numeric count data with an LSTM-based model.
